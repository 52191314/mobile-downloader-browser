Privacy Policy — Aurora: Browser & Downloader

Last updated: 2026-08-08

App: Aurora: Browser & Downloader

Contact: Xianspired@gmail.com

1. What the App is

Aurora: Browser & Downloader is a private web browser and download manager. It lets you browse the web in an in-app browser, detect media and file links, and download files to your device. Optional features include a one-time Aurora Pro or Ultra unlock via Google Play Billing.

2. Information we process

2.1 On your device

Depending on use, the App may store locally:

  • Browser tabs, addresses, and session data
  • History, favorites, or saved pages (if used)
  • Download queue, file names, paths, and progress
  • App settings and preferences
  • WebView cookies and site data for websites you choose to visit
  • Local backups or export files you create

We do not operate a default cloud analytics product that harvests this data from your phone.

2.2 Purchases & License Verification (Aurora Pro / Ultra)

Optional Aurora Pro or Ultra is sold through Google Play Billing. We do not receive your full payment card details. For Google Play distribution builds, the App sends purchase receipt tokens (purchaseToken) and an app install ID (installId) over secure HTTPS to our automated license verification host (https://aurora-license-server.fly.dev) to verify active entitlements via the Google Play Developer API and issue signed license keys.

No browsing history, download URLs, or personal files are ever transmitted to or processed by the license verification host.

2.3 Websites you visit

The built-in browser can open third-party sites. Those sites may collect data under their own policies. We do not control third-party websites.

2.4 Optional Google Drive sync

If you choose to enable Google Drive sync (Settings → Drive):

  • You sign in with your Google account via Google Sign-In. We receive your account name and email address from Google to connect the App to your Drive.
  • Files the App creates — completed downloads and private-vault media you choose to sync — are uploaded to a dedicated Aurora folder in your own Google Drive. Nothing else in your Drive is read or modified.
  • You control the schedule (instant, 15 minutes, hourly, daily). Daily sync counts are capped by tier (free 15 / Pro 50 / Ultra 1,000 per day).
  • Your Google account data and Drive files are handled by Google under Google's own terms and privacy policy; we do not receive or store them on our servers.

3. How we use information

Only to provide browser and download features, save preferences, verify valid Google Play purchases and activate Pro/Ultra features, and run downloads reliably (notifications / foreground service).

We do not sell your personal information. We do not show third-party ads in the App.

5. Data sharing

Shared only as needed: Google Play & License Host (https://aurora-license-server.fly.dev) to confirm purchase receipts and unlock features; Google (Drive sync) — only if you enable it, your Google account identity and the files Aurora creates are shared with Google so they can be stored in your own Drive; and websites you choose to visit. We do not sell data to brokers or advertisers.

6. Retention and deletion

On-device data remains until you clear app data, delete files, or uninstall. Google Drive: files synced to your own Drive remain there until you delete them — the App cannot delete files from your Drive. Disconnect Drive in Settings to stop future syncs. License verification records are stored securely on the verification host to support purchase restoration across app reinstalls and multi-device use.

7. Children

Aurora: Browser & Downloader is not directed at children under 13. It includes an unrestricted web browser and is intended for a general adult audience.

8. Security

We use standard platform mechanisms (HTTPS for network APIs, Android app sandbox, MediaStore for shared downloads). No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.
