Privacy Policy — Aurora Downloader

Last updated: 2026-07-17

App: Aurora Downloader (Android)
Package: com.personal.aurora_downloader
Contact: Use the developer email on the Google Play store listing for this app.

1. What the App is

Aurora Downloader is a private web browser and download manager. It lets you browse the web in an in-app browser, detect media and file links, and download files to your device. Optional features include Google Drive backup/sync and a one-time Aurora Pro unlock via Google Play Billing.

2. Information we process

2.1 On your device

Depending on use, the App may store locally:

  • Browser tabs, addresses, and session data
  • History, favorites, or saved pages (if used)
  • Download queue, file names, paths, and progress
  • App settings and preferences
  • WebView cookies and site data for websites you choose to visit
  • Local backups or export files you create

We do not operate a default cloud analytics product that harvests this data from your phone.

2.2 Optional Google account (Drive)

If you connect Google Drive, sign-in is handled by Google Sign-In. We use access tokens only to sync backup data you enable. Google’s privacy policy applies to your Google account: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Drive is optional and not required for core browsing or downloading.

2.3 Purchases (Aurora Pro)

Optional Aurora Pro is sold through Google Play Billing. We do not receive your full payment card details. Google may share purchase status needed to unlock Pro.

2.4 Websites you visit

The built-in browser can open third-party sites. Those sites may collect data under their own policies. We do not control third-party websites.

3. How we use information

Only to provide browser and download features, save preferences, optional Drive sync, unlock Pro after a valid purchase, and run downloads reliably (notifications / foreground service).

We do not sell your personal information. We do not show third-party ads in the App.

5. Data sharing

Shared only as needed: Google (Sign-In/Drive if you connect them; Play Billing if you purchase); websites you choose to visit. We do not sell data to brokers or advertisers.

6. Retention and deletion

On-device data remains until you clear app data, delete files, or uninstall. Drive files remain in your Drive until you delete them there. Disconnect Drive in settings to stop cloud access.

7. Children

Aurora Downloader is not directed at children under 13. It includes an unrestricted web browser and is intended for a general adult audience.

8. Security

We use standard platform mechanisms (HTTPS for network APIs, Android app sandbox, MediaStore for shared downloads). No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact email on the Google Play store listing for Aurora Downloader.
