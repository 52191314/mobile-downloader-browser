Aurora Download Manager is a supercharged private web browser and fast video downloader built for serious file downloads, torrents, and media capture.

ALL-IN-ONE DOWNLOAD MANAGER & ACCELERATOR
- High-speed multi-threaded file downloader with parallel parts (up to 32 parts on Pro, 64 on Ultra)
- Smart download accelerator that speeds up file, video, and torrent transfers
- Reliable background download manager: pause, resume, and pick up broken links after app restarts or network drops
- Dead-link revival: refresh expired download URLs automatically

SMART WEB VIDEO DOWNLOADER & MEDIA SAVER
- Powerful web video downloader that detects downloadable videos, audio, and media streams as you browse
- Built-in video player with speed control, gesture seek, and Picture-in-Picture (PiP) to preview files before downloading
- Stream & playlist downloader: reassembles m3u8 and video streams into full offline MP4 files
- One-tap batch video downloader: grab multiple media files and links simultaneously
- Auto-organize: downloaded videos, music, and documents are automatically sorted into organized folders

PRIVATE BROWSER WITH BUILT-IN ADBLOCK
- Fast private browser with automatic ad blocker and popup blocker for clean, secure browsing
- Multi-tab browsing with tab groups, bookmarks, and persistent session recovery
- Reader mode, offline page saver, and custom element hiding for distraction-free reading
- Incognito privacy: your browsing history and downloaded files stay strictly on your local device

NATIVE TORRENT DOWNLOADER & MAGNET CLIENT
- Built-in torrent client to download torrents and magnet links directly to your device

AUTOMATIC GOOGLE DRIVE CLOUD BACKUP
