# Aurora Downloader v1.2.0 (Build 76)

## New Features
* **Picture-in-picture mini-player**: Leaving a video in picture-in-picture now keeps it playing in a draggable mini-player over the browser. Tap it to expand back to the full player — the video never stops.
* **Link preview**: Long-press a link and choose "Preview" to check a page before committing to it. Back button or edge-swipe closes the preview and returns instantly — the page underneath is not reloaded.
* Starting a new full-screen video automatically closes the mini-player to avoid overlapping audio.

## Other
* App name corrected to "Aurora Download Manager" (matches the Play listing; shown under the app icon).
