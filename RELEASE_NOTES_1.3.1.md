# Aurora Downloader v1.3.1 (Build 80)

## New Features & Enhancements

* **Video Library & Favorite Videos**: Dedicated video favorites collection with one-tap playback, instant star toggling in custom and mini players, and source webpage navigation.
* **Granular Backup Export**: Selectively choose what to include in backup exports (Web Bookmarks, Favorite Videos, Web History, Saved Pages, Download Queue, Settings, Tabs, and Download Rules).
* **Source Page Jumps**: Easily open original hosting webpages directly from video favorites and watch history items.
* **Monetization & Free Taste Alignment**: Refined Pro/Ultra feature tiering and clear in-app capability breakdowns.

## Improvements

* **Player Star Toggle**: Instant synchronization of favorite video status between player UI and local storage.
* **Watch History Safeguards**: Read-only history protection for free tier users with rolling recents, and full history management for Pro/Ultra tiers.
* **Documentation Update**: Comprehensive user guide and Pro/Ultra feature matrix revisions across all supported languages.
